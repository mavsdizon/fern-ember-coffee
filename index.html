About us

Started by two former night-shift students.

Maya and Daniel met studying for exams in a café that closed at 6 pm. They spent the next three years wishing for a place with good coffee, real tables and enough outlets for everyone. In 2021 they opened it themselves.

Today Fern & Ember is where study groups meet, freelancers set up for the day and neighbors stop by for a quick flat white. Stay for ten minutes or six hours — nobody will rush you.
